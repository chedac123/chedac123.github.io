Graphics and Multimedia

There has been an opportunity to gain information on the use of graphics and multimedia to create visual effects using different types of media such as print media and Web media. Some of the areas covered include image creation and editing, the use of color models like RGB and CMYK and the importance of image resolution. Also there was an opportunity to learn the importance of typography and layout to communication in graphic design. There has also been an opportunity to gain knowledge on different aspects of multimedia including sound, video, animation and interaction. Also there has been an opportunity to learn the importance of CSS to Website design.
