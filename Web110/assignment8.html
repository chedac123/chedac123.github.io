The importance of online security cannot be overstated, as it is critical for maintaining one's privacy and identity in today's highly connected world. In fact, each login, post, and message posted online leaves personal information available for hackers, scams, and malware, thus emphasizing the significance of secure passwords, two-factor authentication, and careful approach to posting and sharing personal details online. At the same time, social media websites gather lots of information about users; therefore, learning privacy settings allows controlling access to personal information, managing posts, and using the information gathered for advertising purposes. Learning how to recognize phishing attempts, scammers' accounts, and links to malicious content lowers the likelihood of identity theft and other types of fraud, whereas careful approach to communicating online prevents users from becoming victims of cyberbullying and spreading misinformation.
