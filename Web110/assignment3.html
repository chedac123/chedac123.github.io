Typically, a search engine is a sophisticated piece of software that can be used or accessed via an accessible website. It can be used to locate information on the internet and is done so by searching through a variety of web pages that are cataloged and categorized to further narrow things down. Basically, people have to use keywords and the search engine uses these key words in order to navigate through databases. This is especially handy whenever people are trying to search for something because there are a variety of factors that come into play when utilizing a search engine. There are a variety of search engines, and they all do the same job, it depends on the software it is downloaded on. Common search engines are Google, Bing, and Internet explorer. Again, while having different and contrasting names, they all have the same functions. Overall, they make life much more efficient due to their fast results and utilities.
