Social media is basically a bunch of platforms that allow people to create, share, and interact with digital content in virtual networks, which shape the flow of communication and information in everyday life. Apps like Instagram and such allow users to upload photos, videos, text and live streams and chat back with likes, comments, shares and messaging. The platforms rely on user generated content and rapid or fast distribution, which allows for easy spreading of information to large audiences and parties . Each algorithm personalizes each feed to user interests and behavior, driving engagement but also raising privacy or misinformation concerns. Social media is also a tool for marketing, activism and community-building, giving people and organizations powerful tools to connect, self-express and participate in online cultural and social conversations.
