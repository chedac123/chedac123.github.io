Internet networking and social networking can be defined as processes involving the creation of connections; however, their purposes are not entirely the same. In internet networking, the goal is technical since the main objective is connecting computers, servers, and devices in order to facilitate the quick and efficient transmission of information all over the world. It is due to the existence of the network of routers, protocols, and data pathways that people can communicate via the exchange of messages, share files, stream videos and use websites. Social networking deals more with human interactions as it offers individuals the chance to communicate online in order to build communities and share their experiences. Some examples of social networking platforms include Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn.
