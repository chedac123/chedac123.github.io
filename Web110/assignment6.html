The importance of communication over the internet is that it enables instantaneous connections regardless of the distance, thus facilitating the rapid exchange of information. Communication over the internet makes it possible to collaborate in educational activities, in professional settings, and in the process of creating. In addition, communication over the internet builds up communities where people can learn together, help each other, and engage in discussion. Finally, as the internet becomes an increasingly integral part of our lives, good skills of communication over the internet will enable people to act safely, effectively, and efficiently.
